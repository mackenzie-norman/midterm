\subsection*{Nyasha's interaction with colonialism}
Nyasha is deeply aware of how colonialism affects her life. She uses food as a metaphor since she has "tasted" what it feels like to not be in Africa. ``Nyasha tucked into the vegetables with the rest of us. When my mother offered her the sour milk she had asked for, she became very morose. She refused to eat''
